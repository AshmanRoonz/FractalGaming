# r/VSPO post: Could Last Ship Sailing be an esport?

*Suggested title:* **Indie dev question for the most esports-literate community I know: could my free browser space-combat game work as an esport? (And will anyone battle me?)**

---

Hey r/VSPO,

First, the honest part: I know this is a fan community for VSPO!, not a game-promotion sub, and I'm a guest here. If the mods feel this doesn't belong, I'll take it down with no hard feelings. I'm posting because VSPO! fans watch more competitive gaming, custom tournaments, and scrims than almost anyone on Reddit, and I have a question that only people like you can really answer.

**The question: what does a game actually need to be a good esport, and does mine have any of it?**

I'm Ashman, a solo indie dev. For the past while I've been building **Last Ship Sailing**, a free zero-gravity 3D ship-combat game that runs entirely in your browser:

**https://lss.fractalreality.ca** (no install, no download, no account required)

The short pitch: seven ships across three chassis, each with its own weapons, ability tree, and shield archetype. The core mode, **LSS Mode**, is round-based last-ship-standing PvP; think a mix of Gunfight from Call of Duty and Last Titan Standing from Titanfall. There's also **Assault** (one fleet defends the Champion Field, the other storms it, roles swap every round), **Race Mode**, a co-op-friendly **Campaign**, and **Exhibition** with per-ship Aegis rank progression. Multiplayer is peer-to-peer through room codes, cross platform, and it even runs in VR on a Quest.

You've watched enough Apex and Valorant customs to know what makes a match watchable: clean readability for spectators, a high skill ceiling, counterplay, comeback potential, moments that clip well. What I'd genuinely love from this community:

1. **Play a match or two and judge it as esports viewers.** Does the round-based last-ship-standing format have tournament bones? Is the ship-vs-ship counterpicking interesting? Can you tell what's happening when you imagine watching instead of playing?
2. **Tell me what's missing.** Spectator mode? Ranked ladder? Better killfeed? If you've ever thought "this game would be a great esport if only...", I want that sentence, applied to mine.
3. **Battle me.** This is the part I really mean: I would love to actually fight people in my own game. Share a room code, bring your friends, and let's find out if a solo dev can defend his own arena. I'm reachable through the LSS Discord (linked from the site).

And yes, the far-off dream scenario is obvious for anyone posting in this sub: custom tournaments with friends, communities, maybe someday streamers and VTubers throwing ships at each other in a browser tab. But that's the dream; right now I just want honest opinions from people who understand competitive games, and a few good matches.

Thanks for reading, and thanks in advance to anyone who gives it ten minutes. Last one sailing wins.

Ashman

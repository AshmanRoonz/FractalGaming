The Game

Last Ship Sailing is 3v3 zero-gravity dogfighting in alien arenas. Pick one of seven ships (Slayer, Tracker, Pyro, Blaster, Vortex, Syphon, Puncture), each with its own weapon, abilities, and Core Move. The round clock ticks down ; the last fleet sailing takes the round.

If both fleets are still alive late in the round, a champion field appears at the heart of the map, sealed inside a massive shell. Crack the shell open, slip your ship inside the field, and the round is yours ; assuming the other fleet doesn't blow you out of it first.

And stay out of the outskirts unless you mean it. Six creatures live beyond the arena walls: the FleshMaw, the GraveTitan, the HallowWalker, the IronBloom, the StoneShroud, and the VoidGazer. They spit lightning, they hit hard, and some of them can teleport right next to you.

Ways To Play

  • Start Single Player ; instant 3v3 against bots. No sign-in, no waiting.
  • Multiplayer ; share a 4-letter room code and your browsers connect directly to each other. Friends, family, the kid down the hall: anyone who enters the code is in your match.
  • Play While Waiting ; host an open match and fight bots while you wait. Other players can drop in mid-round and take over a bot's seat on the enemy team. No lobbies, no queues ; the fight just gets more real.
  • Race Mode ; swap the guns-first arenas for race maps and chase clean lines instead of kills.
  • Anywhere ; keyboard and mouse, touch screen, gamepad, or a VR headset. Same game, same matches.

Signing in with Discord is optional. It adds your name and avatar to matches, the live lobby, stats, and the leaderboard ; the game itself never needs an account.

Make It Yours

The same tools we use to build the game ship with it. Map Lab builds arenas, Sound Lab tunes every effect in the game, and FX Lab plays with the visual effects ; all three are linked on the main screen, all three export settings the game can load. Run a local copy and you can swap in your own ship models, cockpit art, and sounds ; the network only syncs the fight, not the art, so your buddy sees a Slayer while you fly a banana. Both views are real.

The code and original assets are proprietary (see LICENSE) ; personalize your own copy freely, share what you make on the Discord, just don't redistribute or sell the game itself.

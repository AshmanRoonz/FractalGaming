Privacy

Fractal Reality Labs · effective 2026-09-29 · version 1.0

The short version: the labs have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no cookies, and we do not collect personal information. Three labs can connect you with other people, but only when you press Connect. One lab can use your camera and microphone, but only when you turn them on, and what they capture never leaves your device.

Who we are

Fractal Reality Labs is a collection of interactive simulations made by Ashman Roonz (Fractal Reality). The labs are web pages hosted at lss.fractalreality.ca; the app opens those same pages.

What your device downloads, and from whom

To show a lab, your device fetches files from these services. As with any website, each one receives your IP address and basic browser information in order to deliver the files, and their own privacy policies apply:

  • Cloudflare hosts the pages.
  • jsDelivr and esm.sh deliver the code libraries some labs use (for example three.js).
  • Google Fonts delivers the typefaces some pages use.

We do not receive, keep or look at any of that information.

Shared rooms (three labs)

World Navigator, Field and Particle Join, and Particle Break World let you fly the same world as other people. This happens only if you type a room code and press Connect.

  • To find the others, the lab contacts public WebTorrent trackers and connection-helper (STUN) servers, then connects to the other people's devices directly.
  • The other people in the room, and those servers, can see your IP address.
  • What is sent: your position, speed and view direction in the shared world. No name, account, message or file is sent.
  • Nothing about the room is stored by us. Leaving the lab ends the connection.

Camera and microphone (Rule 0)

The lab Rule 0: Substrate Potential can use your camera and microphone as inputs to the simulation, only after you turn them on and your device asks your permission. The video (a tiny 160 by 120 pixel feed) and the sound are analyzed on your device, moment to moment, to drive the simulation. They are never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. Turning the input off, or closing the lab, stops them.

What is saved on your device

Rule 0 saves the patterns it has learned in your browser's local storage, so the next visit can pick up where you left off. The app also keeps one copy of its "you're offline" page, so it can show it without a connection. Nothing else is saved. You can erase both at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser or app settings.

Children

The labs do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

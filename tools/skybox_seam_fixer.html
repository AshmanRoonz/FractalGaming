How it works: the app horizontally shifts the image by half its width so the wrap seam lands in the middle, blends across that center strip, then shifts the image back. This keeps the seam-fix localized where the wrap happens.

Tip: if the result looks smeared, lower the blend width. If the seam is still obvious, raise it. Dramatic objects crossing the seam, like the sun or a rainbow endpoint, are harder to repair perfectly.

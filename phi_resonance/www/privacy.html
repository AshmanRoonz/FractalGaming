Privacy

Phi Resonance · effective 2026-09-29 · version 1.0

The short version: Phi Resonance collects nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app makes no network connections.

Who we are

Phi Resonance is made by Ashman Roonz (Fractal Reality, fractalreality.ca).

What the app does with your data

  • Nothing leaves your device. All sound is generated on the device; the app sends and receives nothing over the internet.
  • Saved states (your presets) are kept in the app's own storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Exports (preset files and wavetables) happen only when you tap an export button. The share sheet then lets you choose where the file goes; we never see it.
  • The app does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Permissions

On Android, the app may ask to show notifications. It uses that only for the notification that appears while a session plays, which carries a Stop button. Saying no does not stop the app from working.

Links

A few links at the bottom of the main screen open fractalreality.ca in your browser. That website has its own privacy practices.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If the app ever starts collecting or sending anything, this page will say so first, with a new effective date. Questions: ashroney@gmail.com.

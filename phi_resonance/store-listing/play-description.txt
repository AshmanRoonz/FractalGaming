Phi Resonance is a sound lab for relaxation, focus, meditation and quiet exploration. Choose a sound, press play, and let it run.

EIGHT SOUND ENGINES
Binaural, isochronic, monaural, drone, harmonic, φ-cascade, noise and chord. Layer up to nine tones spaced by the golden ratio, by octaves, fifths or other intervals, then shape them with tremolo, vibrato, a filter, reverb and textures such as rain, ocean and wind.

THE COMPOSER
Music grown from your sound. The Composer writes a piece around your base tone, with its tempo locked to your beat, its sections following the Fibonacci numbers, and its peak at the golden section. Five styles, from Ambient Field to Golden Bells. Save any piece as an audio file.

BREATHE
A breath guide to follow at your own pace, from 3 to 12 breaths a minute. Keep it on the main screen, open it in its own view, or let it ride over the visuals.

VISUAL BEAT
Colour that pulses in step with the beat, full screen or inside the main window. The app shows a photosensitivity warning first: if you have epilepsy or a history of seizures, do not use the flashing visuals.

MADE FOR LONG SESSIONS
• Presets to start from, and your own settings saved on your phone
• A session timer of up to 2 hours, with a gentle fade at the end
• Plays with the screen locked: one tap prepares a session for it
• Works offline

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Phi Resonance collects no data and makes no network requests.

Headphones are recommended for the binaural modes.

Phi Resonance is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. For any health concern, talk to a healthcare professional.

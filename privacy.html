Last Ship Sailing

Privacy Policy

Effective: May 2, 2026 ; Version 1.0

This Privacy Policy describes how Last Ship Sailing ("LSS," "the Game," "we") handles your data. LSS is a hobby project by Ashman Roonz, hosted at lss.fractalreality.ca.

The short version: LSS does not run a database. We do not store your data on any server we operate. The only data we touch is what you explicitly authorize through Discord OAuth, and even that is only used in the moment to render the Game and post your match results to Discord channels you've joined. Discord stores those posts; we do not.

1. Data we collect via Discord OAuth

When you click "Sign in with Discord" in LSS, Discord prompts you to authorize the Game to read a small set of data about your Discord account. We request only the identify scope (and, if you launch LSS as a Discord Activity in a voice channel, additional scopes specific to the Activity context).

The data we receive from Discord under identify consists of:

  • Your Discord user ID (a numeric identifier).
  • Your Discord username and global display name.
  • Your Discord avatar (URL to the image hosted by Discord).

We do not request:

  • Your email address.
  • Your phone number.
  • Your IP address (Discord may log this on its side; we do not see it).
  • Your direct message contents or any private channel content.
  • Connections to other accounts (Twitch, YouTube, etc.) unless you explicitly grant additional scopes in a future feature.

2. How we use that data

Your Discord identity is used for:

  • Rendering your avatar and display name in the Game's lobby and (planned) in-match HUD elements.
  • Attaching your identity to match results posted to Discord channels in our community server (so leaderboards know who played).
  • Distinguishing your match-result posts from other players' posts during the consensus-validation step that determines whether a match counts.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, training of AI models, or any commercial purpose. LSS is a non-commercial hobby project.

3. Where your data is stored

This is the unusual part of LSS's privacy story, and it's worth explaining clearly.

On your device (browser localStorage): Your Discord OAuth access token and a cached copy of your Discord user object are stored in your browser's localStorage so signin persists across page reloads. This data lives on your machine only. Clearing your browser's site data, or signing out of LSS, removes it.

On Discord (channel messages): Match results posted by LSS appear as messages in Discord channels we operate (e.g. #leaderboards, #match-records). Discord stores those messages indefinitely under its own data policies. To delete them, you can ask a moderator in the LSS Discord server to remove specific posts, or revoke your authorization to delete future posts.

On Cloudflare Workers (transient relay): When the Game posts a match result, the request flows through a Cloudflare Worker (lss-discord-proxy.ashroney.workers.dev). The Worker validates your Discord token, sets the post's display avatar/name from your Discord identity, then relays the message to Discord. The Worker does not log requests or persist any user data; it is stateless beyond the encrypted webhook URLs it holds as configuration secrets. Cloudflare may log the request as part of its own infrastructure logging; see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy for what they do with that.

On any server we operate: Nothing. We do not run a database, an analytics service, an account system, or any server-side data store. There is no LSS-side record of who you are or what you've done in the Game beyond what lives on your device and on Discord.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

LSS does not use cookies. We use browser localStorage to persist your Discord token (described above) and your in-game settings (volume preferences, control bindings, etc.). We do not use third-party analytics, advertising trackers, fingerprinting, or any other tracking mechanism.

5. How to delete your data

You have several straightforward options to remove your data from LSS:

  • Sign out from the LSS lobby. This clears your Discord token and cached identity from your browser's localStorage immediately.
  • Revoke LSS's access in your Discord account settings, under Advanced → Authorized Apps. This invalidates any token LSS holds for you and prevents future signins until you re-authorize.
  • Clear your browser site data for lss.fractalreality.ca. Removes all locally stored LSS data including settings.
  • Delete past match-result posts by asking a moderator in the LSS Discord server. Since the posts live on Discord, deletion happens through Discord's interface.

6. Children's privacy

LSS is not directed at children under 13. You must be at least 13 years old (or the minimum age required by Discord in your jurisdiction, whichever is higher) to use LSS. If we become aware that a user under that age has signed in, we will revoke their access.

7. Third parties

LSS interacts with the following third-party services:

  • Discord (identity, channel storage, planned Activities runtime). Subject to Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare (DNS, edge serving, Worker proxy). Subject to Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages (static hosting for the Game and these documents). Subject to GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • WebTorrent / BitTorrent trackers (used by Trystero for peer discovery during multiplayer matchmaking). These are public infrastructure not operated by us; they see your peer ID briefly during the connection handshake.
  • Google Fonts (used by these documentation pages, not by the Game itself). Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not control these services and we are not responsible for their privacy practices. We chose them for technical fit and intentionally use only the minimum surface area each provides.

8. Data we do NOT collect

To be clear about what's not happening:

  • No analytics or telemetry.
  • No advertising identifiers.
  • No browser fingerprinting.
  • No background data collection.
  • No selling, renting, sharing, or licensing of your data to anyone.
  • No use of your data to train AI models.

9. International users

LSS is operated from Canada. By using LSS, you consent to your data being processed in Canada and routed through Cloudflare's global edge network. If you are a resident of the European Union, the United Kingdom, California, or another jurisdiction with specific data-protection laws, you may have additional rights (access, correction, deletion, portability). The minimal-data architecture of LSS makes most of these straightforward to honor; the deletion mechanisms in Section 5 satisfy the typical requests.

10. Security

We follow standard security practices for the limited surface area LSS exposes:

  • All connections use HTTPS (provided by Cloudflare and GitHub Pages).
  • The Cloudflare Worker validates Discord tokens before relaying any post.
  • Webhook URLs are stored as encrypted Worker secrets, never embedded in client code.
  • The Game uses Discord's PKCE OAuth flow, which avoids the need for any shared client secret.

That said, no system is perfectly secure. If you become aware of a security issue with LSS, please report it privately to ashroney@gmail.com rather than disclosing it publicly.

12. Contact
